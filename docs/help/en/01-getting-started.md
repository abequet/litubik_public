# Getting started

LitUbik guides a systematic literature review from protocol to PRISMA diagram,
with multiple reviewers and optional AI assistance.

## First steps

1. **Create a project** from the home page (*New project*). The person who
   creates it becomes its **admin**. Right after creation, a short **access
   setup** walks you through the three optional kinds of access: your
   institutional library access (for paywalled PDFs), database API keys
   (Scopus, IEEE…) and AI keys. Every step can be skipped and configured
   later in the project settings.
2. **Invite your co-reviewers**: on the project card, open *Members* and add
   them by email. If they have no account yet, the invitation activates when
   they register with that address.
3. **Follow the pipeline** in the left sidebar: Protocol → Equations → Search →
   Dedup → Screening → FT retrieval → FT screening → Snowballing → Extraction →
   Results → PRISMA. Each step unlocks naturally as the previous one
   progresses; the highlighted number shows where you are.

## Roles

- **App superadmin** — the first account created on the instance. Manages
  global settings (app-wide AI and database keys, in the *Administration*
  section of the sidebar).
- **Project admin** — manages members, settings, AI keys and prompts of a
  project (⚙ button on the project card or *Settings* in the sidebar), and
  sees the active work time of each reviewer per stage (*Work time* page).
- **Reviewer** — screens, votes, extracts data; sees their own work time on
  the Members panel (see *Collaboration*).

## The guided tour

On your first login a short guided tour introduces the interface. You can
relaunch it any time from the **?** menu in the header.
