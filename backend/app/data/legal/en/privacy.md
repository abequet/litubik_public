# Privacy notice

## Who is responsible

## Guest sessions: no personal data asked

A guest session is opened without any name, e-mail address or password: the
account gets a random identifier, and a password nobody knows.

## What is processed, why, and for how long

- **The guest session and what you create in it** (projects, references,
  files, votes, notes). Purpose: to provide the demonstration. Legal basis:
  performance of the [terms of use](/legal/terms) (GDPR art. 6(1)(b)).
  Kept until the daily reset at {{reset_hour}} UTC, or until you end the
  session.
- **Your IP address.** Purpose: to limit abuse (how many sessions an address
  may open, sign-in attempts). Legal basis: legitimate interest in the
  security of the service (art. 6(1)(f)). Kept in memory only, 24 hours at
  most, never written to the database.
- **The host's technical logs** (IP address, date, requested address).
  Purpose: running and securing the service. Legal basis: legitimate interest
  (art. 6(1)(f)). Kept as long as the host keeps them.
- **Messages sent with the feedback button.** Purpose: improving the
  software. Legal basis: legitimate interest (art. 6(1)(f)). Kept after the
  session ends, without any link to it.
- **AI spending.** Purpose: enforcing the caps of the demonstration. Per
  session: deleted with it. The monthly total is an amount without any
  personal data.
- **Accounts of invited collaborators** (e-mail address, display name, their
  work). Purpose: giving them access to their projects. Legal basis:
  performance of the terms (art. 6(1)(b)). Kept until the account is deleted
  at their request.

No data is sold or used for advertising. There is no analytics and no
tracker.

## Who receives data

- **The host**, {{host_name}} ({{host_address}}), which stores the instance.
  When the host is established outside the European Union, the transfer relies
  on the safeguards of its data processing agreement (standard contractual
  clauses).
- **AI providers**, only when you use an AI feature: the text concerned
  (titles, abstracts, criteria, document extracts) is sent to the provider the
  operator configured (for instance Anthropic, OpenAI, Google or Mistral),
  which may be established outside the European Union. Your identity is not
  sent.
- **Open scholarly services** (Crossref, Unpaywall, OpenAlex, PubMed, Europe
  PMC…) receive the DOIs and search queries needed, with the operator's
  contact address — never yours.

## Cookies and local storage

The instance sets a single cookie, strictly necessary: the session cookie
that keeps you signed in ({{session_hours}} hours at most). Your browser also
keeps your interface language and whether you have seen the guided tour
(local storage). Being strictly necessary, they need no consent. There is no
other cookie.

## Your rights

You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction and
objection (GDPR art. 15 to 21). The quickest erasure: *End session and delete
my data*, in the user menu, deletes everything at once. For anything else:
{{contact_email}}. You may also lodge a complaint with the CNIL
(<https://www.cnil.fr>) or with the supervisory authority of your country.
